# 🌍 The Problem

Legal information is often difficult for ordinary citizens to understand.

People frequently struggle with:

* IPC Sections
* FIR Procedures
* RTI Applications
* Cyber Crime Complaints
* Consumer Rights
* Employment Laws
* POSH Guidelines

Most legal resources are written using complex terminology, making them difficult to interpret without professional assistance.

---

# 💡 The Solution

**JurixAI** is an AI-powered legal assistant specifically designed for the Indian Legal System.

Unlike generic chatbots, JurixAI combines Large Language Models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide:

✅ Context-aware responses

✅ Reduced hallucinations

✅ Trusted legal information retrieval

✅ Hinglish & English conversations

✅ Empathetic legal guidance

✅ Easy-to-understand explanations

Think of JurixAI as your **Digital Legal Saathi**.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

### ⚖️ Legal Context Awareness

Uses legal documents as the primary source of truth rather than relying solely on model memory.

### 🧠 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Retrieves relevant legal context before generating responses, improving factual accuracy.

### 🌐 Hinglish + English Support

Users can communicate naturally using English, Hinglish, or mixed-language conversations.

### ❤️ Empathy-First Design

Designed to communicate respectfully, calmly, and clearly during stressful legal situations.

### 📚 Structured Legal Responses

Every answer focuses on:

* Explanation
* Relevant legal concepts
* Practical next steps
* Easy-to-understand language

### 🛡️ SonarQube Quality Analysis

Integrated with SonarQube to detect bugs, code smells, security vulnerabilities, and enforce Quality Gates before deployment.

### ☸️ Kubernetes Native

Supports production deployment using Docker and Kubernetes.
